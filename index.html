About

I am a tenured associate professor at the Institute for Economic and Social Research (IESR), Jinan University. My research focuses on urban economics and environmental economics, with a particular emphasis on the intersection of the two fields. My recent work examines the impacts and underlying mechanisms of urban and environmental policies on land markets, firm polluting behavior, and regional environmental quality.
